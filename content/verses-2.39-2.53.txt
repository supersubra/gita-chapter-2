# Part 4: Do your best, let go of the result (2.39–2.53)
intro: This is the heart of the chapter. Krishna teaches how to work with full effort and a light heart, without being tied to marks, praise or rewards.

## 2.39
sk: एषा तेऽभिहिता साङ्ख्ये बुद्धिर्योगे त्विमां शृणु । बुद्ध्या युक्तो यया पार्थ कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यसि ॥
read: eshaa te 'bhihitaa saankhye buddhir yoge tv imaam shrinu | buddhyaa yukto yayaa paartha karma-bandham prahaasyasi
t: From knowing to doing
one: Knowing something is the first half. Now learn how to live it.
meaning: "So far I have given you this wisdom as knowledge. Now hear it as a way of acting. With this understanding, Arjuna, you will be free from the bondage of action."
gem: Mindful action (putting what I know into practice)
s: The advice you give but do not follow | You tell your friend, "Don't check your phone while studying," and she follows it and improves. Your own phone is still on your desk. She laughs and says, "Take your own medicine." You put the phone in the next room.
s: The fitness plan | You and your roommate have watched twenty videos about exercise and made a perfect chart. Neither of you has run once. One morning he knocks on your door at six and says, "Enough planning. Shoes."
s: Your father's lessons | Your father is also your music teacher. He has taught you and your sister every evening for years, and now he expects both of you to practise on your own, without being called. He knows how much time and effort you have both put in and how good you have become, and he does not want that talent to go to waste. You know exactly what to practise, and still you wait to be told. One morning you hear your sister already singing in the next room. You sit down and begin.
h: **Pick one thing you already know is right** and do it today.
h: **Practise with a friend.** Check on each other once a week.
h: **Keep it small.** One habit that you really do is better than ten that you only plan.
try: Choose one piece of good advice you have heard many times, and follow it for one day.
bhakti: Krishna does not stop at explaining. He shows Arjuna how to live it. Devotion also grows by practice, a little every day.

## 2.40
sk: नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते । स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात् ॥
read: nehaabhikrama-naasho 'sti pratyavaayo na vidyate | svalpam apy asya dharmasya traayate mahato bhayaat
t: No effort is wasted
one: No honest effort is ever wasted, and even a little practice protects you from great fear.
meaning: "On this path no effort is lost, and there is no harm in trying. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear."
gem: Mindful action (small steps count)
s: Ten minutes a day | You are weak at maths and feel it is too late to catch up. Your friend says, "Give me ten minutes after school every day." It seems too little to matter. After two months, you are no longer afraid of the maths period.
s: The friend learning to swim | Your friend is ashamed that she cannot swim at nineteen. She goes to the pool twice a week and can only float. "It's nothing," she says. But the day your group goes to the river, she is the one who is not afraid.
s: The unfinished course | You started an evening course and left it halfway when work got busy. You count it as a failure. A year later, the half you learned is exactly what a new project needs.
h: **Start small,** with five or ten minutes.
h: **Do not count a broken streak as a loss.** What you practised is still yours.
h: **Begin again without scolding yourself.**
try: Do ten minutes of something you gave up on.
bhakti: God counts every small step taken toward the good. Even one sincere prayer or one kind act is kept safe with Him.

## 2.41
sk: व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिरेकेह कुरुनन्दन । बहुशाखा ह्यनन्ताश्च बुद्धयोऽव्यवसायिनाम् ॥
read: vyavasaayaatmikaa buddhir ekeha kuru-nandana | bahu-shaakhaa hy anantaash cha buddhayo 'vyavasaayinaam
t: One clear aim
one: A decided mind has one aim. An undecided mind runs along endless branches.
meaning: "Those who are firm in purpose have a single aim, Arjuna. The thoughts of the undecided are many-branched and endless."
gem: Mindful action (one thing at a time)
s: Six tabs open | You sit down to study history. Within ten minutes you have a chat open, a video paused, a game loading and the textbook somewhere underneath. Your friend on the call says, "Close everything. Twenty-five minutes, only history. I will do the same."
s: The friend with five plans | Your friend wants to prepare for civil services, start a channel, learn guitar and apply abroad, all this year. By March he has done a little of each and is tired. You ask, "If you could finish only one, which?" He goes quiet, then answers.
s: Too many projects | You have said yes to everything at work, and nothing is finished. A colleague you trust looks at your list and says, "Choose the one that matters most this week. Tell people the rest will wait."
h: **Choose one aim for this hour.** Close the rest.
h: **Choose one main aim for this term or year,** and let the smaller ones fit around it.
h: **Ask a friend to hold you to it.**
try: Do one task for twenty-five minutes with every other screen and tab closed.
bhakti: A mind with one aim is a quiet place. In that quiet, it is easier to remember God.

## 2.42
sk: यामिमां पुष्पितां वाचं प्रवदन्त्यविपश्चितः । वेदवादरताः पार्थ नान्यदस्तीति वादिनः ॥
read: yaam imaam pushpitaam vaacham pravadanty avipashchitah | veda-vaada-rataah paartha naanyad asteeti vaadinah
t: Flowery words
one: Do not be carried away by flowery promises. Check what lies behind them.
meaning: "Those with little understanding speak flowery words, Arjuna. They delight in the letter of the scriptures and say, 'There is nothing else.'"
gem: Mindful action (seeing through big promises)
s: "Score 95 in thirty days" | An advertisement promises top marks in a month with a secret method. Your friend is ready to pay with his birthday money. You both ask a senior who topped last year. She laughs and says, "The secret is the textbook, twice."
s: The friend who talks big | A new friend in college talks of rich contacts and sure-shot plans and wants you to put money into his scheme. He speaks beautifully. You say, "Let me ask my father first." His plan cannot answer your father's three plain questions.
s: The shiny job offer | A company promises you a grand title and "unlimited growth." A friend who once worked there tells you what the days are really like. You are glad you asked before signing.
h: **Be careful when something sounds too good.** Ask, "What is the plain truth here?"
h: **Ask someone who has actually done it.**
h: **Take a day before you decide,** and talk to a parent or an elder.
try: Find one advertisement or post that promises too much, and work out what it leaves unsaid.
bhakti: Krishna warns that even holy words can be used to chase rewards. Real devotion is simple and does not need decoration.

## 2.43
sk: कामात्मानः स्वर्गपरा जन्मकर्मफलप्रदाम् । क्रियाविशेषबहुलां भोगैश्वर्यगतिं प्रति ॥
read: kaamaatmaanah svarga-paraa janma-karma-phala-pradaam | kriyaa-vishesha-bahulaam bhogaishvarya-gatim prati
t: Is the prize the only reason?
one: When the prize becomes the only reason, even good actions turn into a trade.
meaning: "Full of desires and aiming only at heaven, they speak of many special rituals that lead to pleasure and power, and to more birth as the fruit of action."
gem: Mindful action (the reason behind what I do)
s: Friends for a reason | A classmate becomes very friendly just before the exams and asks for your notes. After the exams she does not sit with you again. It hurts. You decide you will never treat friendship as a way to get something.
s: Volunteering for the certificate | You join a clean-up drive because the certificate will look good. Your friend joins because she likes the lake. By noon you are tired and bored, and she is still cheerful. You see what a difference the reason makes.
s: Helping only the boss | A colleague is helpful to senior people and cold to everyone else. People notice. You make a point of helping the new intern, who can do nothing for you in return.
h: **Ask, "Why am I really doing this?"**
h: **Do one good thing with no reward attached,** not even praise.
h: **Value friends for who they are,** not for what they can give you.
try: Help someone today and do not tell anyone about it.
bhakti: Prayer is not a shop where we pay and collect. The sweetest prayer asks for nothing and simply stays near God.

## 2.44
sk: भोगैश्वर्यप्रसक्तानां तयापहृतचेतसाम् । व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिः समाधौ न विधीयते ॥
read: bhogaishvarya-prasaktaanaam tayaapahrita-chetasaam | vyavasaayaatmikaa buddhih samaadhau na vidheeyate
t: When the mind is carried off
one: A mind that is always chasing pleasure and status cannot settle on anything.
meaning: "Those who cling to pleasure and power, and whose minds are carried away by such talk, cannot gain the steady, single-pointed mind."
gem: Evenness (a settled mind)
s: The new phone | Your friend gets the latest phone, and for a week you can think of nothing else. In class you plan how to convince your parents. You notice you have not heard a word of two whole chapters.
s: Counting followers | You and a friend start posting together. Soon you are checking your follower count between lectures and feeling low when his grows faster. One evening you both agree to leave your phones in the room and go out for a walk.
s: The next big thing | You bought a bike last year, and it made you happy for a month. Now your friends talk about cars, and the bike seems small. You wonder whether the car will be any different after a month.
h: **Notice when wanting something has taken over your mind.**
h: **Wait a week** before asking for or buying what you suddenly want.
h: **Spend time with friends on things that cost nothing,** such as a walk, a game or a talk.
try: Write down one thing you badly wanted last year. Does it still make you as happy?
bhakti: A mind pulled in many directions cannot sit with God for even a minute. A simple life makes room for Him.

## 2.45
sk: त्रैगुण्यविषया वेदा निस्त्रैगुण्यो भवार्जुन । निर्द्वन्द्वो नित्यसत्त्वस्थो निर्योगक्षेम आत्मवान् ॥
read: traigunya-vishayaa vedaa nistraigunyo bhavaarjuna | nirdvandvo nitya-sattva-stho niryoga-kshema aatmavaan
t: Rooted in yourself
one: Rise above the pull of likes and dislikes, stop worrying about getting and keeping, and stay rooted in yourself.
meaning: "The scriptures deal with the three qualities of nature. Rise above them, Arjuna. Be free from the pairs of opposites, stay steady in goodness, do not worry about gaining and keeping, and be rooted in the Self."
gem: Evenness (free of the tug-of-war)
s: Liked today, ignored tomorrow | On Monday the popular group calls you to sit with them, and you feel wonderful. On Thursday they ignore you, and you feel terrible. Your old friend, who has sat beside you since you were both small, says, "You are the same person on both days."
s: Keeping your place | You finally make it into the college dance team, and now you are afraid of losing your place. You practise from worry, not from joy. A senior says, "Dance like the day you auditioned. You had nothing to protect then."
s: Holding on | You have a good job and good savings, yet you lie awake working out how to keep it all safe. A friend with far less sleeps soundly. You ask him how. "I do today's work," he says. "The rest was never in my hands."
h: **Notice the swing** between "they like me" and "they don't."
h: **Do what is good,** whether or not it is noticed.
h: **Hold what you have with an open hand.** Look after it, but do not let fear guard it.
try: Catch one moment today when your mood depended on someone's reaction, and take a slow breath.
bhakti: The one who is rooted in the Self trusts God for getting and keeping. Later in the Gita He promises to carry that load Himself.

## 2.46
sk: यावानर्थ उदपाने सर्वतः सम्प्लुतोदके । तावान्सर्वेषु वेदेषु ब्राह्मणस्य विजानतः ॥
read: yaavaan artha udapaane sarvatah samplutodake | taavaan sarveshu vedeshu braahmanasya vijaanatah
t: The well and the lake
one: When you have found the real thing, the smaller things you chased matter less, like a well beside a great lake.
meaning: "When water is flooding everywhere, a small well is of little use. In the same way, all the scriptures are of little use to the wise person who knows the truth."
gem: My steady Self (finding the source)
s: Guide books and understanding | Your friend has every guide book and question bank. You have understood the chapter properly from the teacher. The night before the test, she is rushing through five books, and you are calm. She asks you to explain it to her.
s: One true friend | In first year you tried hard to be part of every group and felt lonely in all of them. In second year you have one friend who truly knows you. You find you no longer need to be invited everywhere.
s: Approval at work | You used to need praise from every senior to feel you were doing well. After two years, you know your work yourself. Praise is still pleasant, but you no longer go looking for it.
h: **Look for understanding,** not only more material.
h: **Value depth.** One real friend, one subject truly learned, one prayer truly meant.
h: **When you feel you need more and more, ask what you are really thirsty for.**
try: Take one topic and explain it to a friend in your own words, without the book.
bhakti: Rituals and books are wells. The love of God is the lake. The wells have done their work when they lead us there.

## 2.47
sk: कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन । मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥
read: karmany evaadhikaaras te maa phaleshu kadaachana | maa karma-phala-hetur bhoor maa te sango 'stv akarmani
t: Your right is to the work
one: The work is in your hands. The result is not. So give your full effort, and never use this as an excuse to do nothing.
meaning: "You have a right to your work alone, never to its fruits. Do not let the fruit be your reason for acting, and do not be drawn to doing nothing."
gem: Mindful action
s: The result you cannot control | You and your friend study together for the board exams. She keeps asking, "What if the paper is tough? What if they mark strictly?" You say, "We cannot set the paper. We can only finish this chapter tonight." You both get back to it.
s: The friend who did not get selected | Your friend practised harder than anyone and was still left out of the team. He says, "So what was the use?" You tell him what you saw: he became a far better player this year. The selection was never in his hands. The practice was.
s: Practice and the prize | Your father has taught you the veena since you were six, and he still listens to your practice every morning. The night before a competition you ask him, "What if I do not win?" He says, "I did not teach you so that you would win. I taught you to play. The judges are not in your hands. This morning's practice was, and you did it well."
h: **Ask, "What part of this is in my hands?"** Do that part fully.
h: **Hand over the rest.** Marks, selection and other people's choices are not yours to control.
h: **Do not swing to the other side** and say, "Then why try?" Not trying is never the answer.
try: Before your next piece of work, write two lists: "In my hands" and "Not in my hands."
bhakti: This is the best-known verse of the Gita. We do the work as an offering, and we receive whatever comes as His prasad.

## 2.48
sk: योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय । सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते ॥
read: yoga-sthah kuru karmaani sangam tyaktvaa dhananjaya | siddhy-asiddhyoh samo bhootvaa samatvam yoga uchyate
t: Evenness is yoga
one: Work with a steady mind, the same in success and in failure. That evenness is what yoga means.
meaning: "Stay steady in yoga and do your work, Arjuna, giving up attachment and remaining the same in success and failure. This evenness is called yoga."
gem: Evenness
s: Results day | You and your three closest friends open your results together. Two did well, one did badly, and you are in the middle. You make a pact: nobody boasts and nobody sulks today. You all go out to eat as planned.
s: Winning and losing in one week | Your college team wins on Tuesday and loses on Friday. After the win, your captain says, "Good. Practice tomorrow at six." After the loss, she says exactly the same words. You begin to see why the team follows her.
s: The praised report, the rejected report | In one month, one of your reports is praised in front of everyone, and another is sent back full of red marks. A friend at the next desk says, "Same person wrote both. Learn from both. Lunch?"
h: **Keep your routine the same** after a win and after a loss.
h: **Celebrate lightly and grieve lightly.** Then return to the work.
h: **Stand by friends in both.** Be glad for the one who won, and sit with the one who lost.
try: The next time you get a result, wait ten minutes before you tell anyone.
bhakti: Yoga is not only postures. Krishna says it is evenness of mind, and a mind like that rests easily in God.

## 2.49
sk: दूरेण ह्यवरं कर्म बुद्धियोगाद्धनञ्जय । बुद्धौ शरणमन्विच्छ कृपणाः फलहेतवः ॥
read: doorena hy avaram karma buddhi-yogaad dhananjaya | buddhau sharanam anvichchha kripanaah phala-hetavah
t: Why you work matters
one: Work done only for reward is poor work. Take shelter in a wiser reason.
meaning: "Work done for reward is far below work done with this understanding, Arjuna. Take refuge in this wisdom. Those who work only for the fruit are to be pitied."
gem: Mindful action (working from a better reason)
s: Studying for the gift | Your parents have promised you a new cycle if you score above ninety. Your friend studies because she loves the subject. In the exam hall, you are tense about the cycle, and she is enjoying the paper. You both notice the difference afterwards.
s: Sharing notes | Before exams, some classmates hide their notes so that others will not score more. Your friend shares hers with anyone who asks. She says, "Explaining it helps me learn it." She scores well, and she is the one everyone is glad for.
s: Working for the appraisal | A colleague works hard only in the two months before appraisals. You notice how anxious he is in those months, and how bored for the rest of the year.
h: **Find a reason inside the work itself.** Learning, helping, or doing it well.
h: **Notice how tense you feel** when a reward is the only reason.
h: **Help a friend do well** even when you are competing with them.
try: Do one task today as well as you can, for no reason except to do it well.
bhakti: The wiser reason Krishna offers is to make our work an offering to Him. Then even small work becomes worship.

## 2.50
sk: बुद्धियुक्तो जहातीह उभे सुकृतदुष्कृते । तस्माद्योगाय युज्यस्व योगः कर्मसु कौशलम् ॥
read: buddhi-yukto jahaateeha ubhe sukrita-dushkrite | tasmaad yogaaya yujyasva yogah karmasu kaushalam
t: Skill in action
one: Yoga is skill in action. A calm mind does the same work better.
meaning: "One who has this understanding leaves behind both good and bad results in this very life. So devote yourself to yoga. Yoga is skill in action."
gem: Mindful action (calm skill)
s: The calm batter | In the school match, your friend is batting with ten runs needed in the last over. Everyone is shouting. He taps his bat, breathes and watches only the ball. Later he tells you, "I was not thinking about winning. I was thinking about the next ball."
s: Cooking for the hostel | It is your turn to cook for eight friends. The first time, you panic about whether they will like it and burn the dal. The next time you think only about each step, and the food is good.
s: The demo | During a live demo, your screen freezes. You feel the panic rise. You say, "Give me a moment," breathe, and restart. A colleague says afterwards, "The freeze was nothing. The way you handled it was what the client noticed."
h: **Bring your mind to the step in front of you,** not to the scoreboard.
h: **Breathe before the important moment.** Three slow breaths are enough.
h: **Practise calm on small things,** so that it is there for the big ones.
try: Do one ordinary task today, such as writing a page or washing a plate, with your full attention.
bhakti: Work done calmly and carefully is itself a prayer. Krishna calls this skill yoga.

## 2.51
sk: कर्मजं बुद्धियुक्ता हि फलं त्यक्त्वा मनीषिणः । जन्मबन्धविनिर्मुक्ताः पदं गच्छन्त्यनामयम् ॥
read: karma-jam buddhi-yuktaa hi phalam tyaktvaa maneeshinah | janma-bandha-vinirmuktaah padam gachchhanty anaamayam
t: Free of the load
one: The wise let go of the results of their work, and so they are free of a great load.
meaning: "The wise, who have this understanding, give up the fruit of their actions. Freed from bondage, they reach a state beyond all sorrow."
gem: Evenness (travelling light)
s: The friend who never keeps score | One of your friends helps everyone and never mentions it again. Another reminds you of every favour. You notice which friend is lighter and happier, and which one you want to be like.
s: After the competition | Your college play did not win. Half the cast is still complaining a week later. Your friend who played the lead says, "We did it well, and I loved doing it. I am not carrying that trophy home in my head."
s: The credit | Your idea is used in a project, and your name is not mentioned. You are upset for a day. Then you decide to let it go and bring the next idea. Your friend says, "You look less tired than the people who fight for every bit of credit."
h: **Do the good thing and drop it.** Do not keep a list of what others owe you.
h: **Let last week's result stay in last week.**
h: **Notice how light you feel** when you stop keeping score.
try: Let go of one old favour or one old result that you keep bringing up.
bhakti: Whatever we hand over to God, we no longer have to carry. That is the freedom Krishna speaks of.

## 2.52
sk: यदा ते मोहकलिलं बुद्धिर्व्यतितरिष्यति । तदा गन्तासि निर्वेदं श्रोतव्यस्य श्रुतस्य च ॥
read: yadaa te moha-kalilam buddhir vyatitarishyati | tadaa gantaasi nirvedam shrotavyasya shrutasya cha
t: Out of the mud
one: When your mind comes out of the mud of confusion, other people's noise loses its hold on you.
meaning: "When your understanding crosses the mud of confusion, you will no longer be troubled by what you have heard or what you may yet hear."
gem: My steady Self (clear thinking)
s: "Everyone is saying" | "Everyone says commerce has no scope." "Everyone says you must take coaching." You and your friend are dizzy with what everyone says. You sit down with her mother, who asks, "What do the two of you want to learn?" The noise becomes quieter.
s: Hostel rumours | The hostel is full of rumours: the exam is postponed, the paper has leaked, placements are cancelled. Your friend checks the notice board and the official mail and ignores the rest. You start doing the same and gain two calm hours a day.
s: The forwarded message | A frightening message about your industry goes around the office groups. Half your friends are ready to resign. You look for the actual source and find that the story is three years old.
h: **Go to the source.** Check the notice, the teacher or the official page.
h: **Limit how much opinion you take in** when you have a decision to make.
h: **Talk it through with one clear-headed friend or elder.**
try: The next time you hear "everyone is saying," ask, "Who exactly, and how do they know?"
bhakti: A clear mind is a gift of grace. We can ask for it every morning: "Lord, let me see things as they are."

## 2.53
sk: श्रुतिविप्रतिपन्ना ते यदा स्थास्यति निश्चला । समाधावचला बुद्धिस्तदा योगमवाप्स्यसि ॥
read: shruti-vipratipannaa te yadaa sthaasyati nishchalaa | samaadhaav achalaa buddhis tadaa yogam avaapsyasi
t: A mind that stays
one: When your mind stops being pulled by conflicting voices and stays steady within, you have reached yoga.
meaning: "When your mind, now confused by all you have heard, stands firm and steady within, then you will reach yoga."
gem: My steady Self (a settled mind)
s: Two friends, two opinions | One friend says, "Take biology." Another says, "Take computers." Each time you talk to one of them, you change your mind. Finally you sit alone for an evening with a notebook. In the quiet, you already know which one you want.
s: The noisy feed | Before every decision, you ask in three groups and watch five videos. You end up more confused. A friend suggests one day with no feed. By evening your own thoughts are clear enough to hear.
s: After all the advice | You have a job choice to make, and you have heard from your parents, friends and mentors. You thank them all. Then you take a long walk by yourself. When you return, you know your answer, and you feel calm about it.
h: **Listen to advice, and then stop collecting it.**
h: **Give yourself quiet time** with no phone before a decision.
h: **Trust the answer that comes when you are calm,** not the one that comes when you are rushed.
try: Sit quietly for five minutes today with no phone, no music and no book.
bhakti: When the mind is still, it rests in God. That stillness is what Krishna calls samadhi.
