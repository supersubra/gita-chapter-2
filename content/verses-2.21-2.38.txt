## 2.21
sk: वेदाविनाशिनं नित्यं य एनमजमव्ययम् । कथं स पुरुषः पार्थ कं घातयति हन्ति कम् ॥
read: vedaavinaashinam nityam ya enam ajam avyayam | katham sa purushah paartha kam ghaatayati hanti kam
t: Nothing can finish you
one: Once you know what cannot be destroyed in you, no single event can make you say "I am finished."
meaning: "Arjuna, a person who knows the Self to be indestructible, eternal, unborn and unchanging cannot truly destroy anyone, or cause anyone to be destroyed."
gem: My steady Self
s: The rumour | A classmate spreads a story about you, and by lunch it feels as if your name is finished. Your best friend sits beside you with her tiffin, the same as every day. "Nothing about you changed this morning," she says. "Only a story went around."
s: The friend who failed a year | Your friend has to repeat a year and tells you, "I am finished. There is no point now." You do not argue. You remind him of the week he taught four of you maths before the exam. "That person did not fail," you say. "One year did."
s: The dropped catch | You drop the last catch, and your street's team loses the final. You are sure nobody will ever pick you again. Your elder brother finds you sitting on the steps. "A catch was dropped. You were not," he says. "Come, I will throw you fifty before dinner."
h: **Separate the event from yourself.** Say "Something went wrong," not "I am finished."
h: **Say the same to a friend** who thinks one failure has ended them.
h: **Ask someone who has known you for years** what has not changed in you.
try: Write one line about something that worried you this week: "This happened. I am still here."
bhakti: The Self in you is God's own gift. No person and no event has the power to take it back.

## 2.22
sk: वासांसि जीर्णानि यथा विहाय नवानि गृह्णाति नरोऽपराणि । तथा शरीराणि विहाय जीर्णान्यन्यानि संयाति नवानि देही ॥

## 2.23
sk: नैनं छिन्दन्ति शस्त्राणि नैनं दहति पावकः । न चैनं क्लेदयन्त्यापो न शोषयति मारुतः ॥
read: nainam chhindanti shastraani nainam dahati paavakah | na chainam kledayanty aapo na shoshayati maarutah
t: What nothing can cut
one: Weapons, fire, water and wind cannot touch the Self, and sharp words cannot either.
meaning: "Weapons cannot cut the Self, fire cannot burn it, water cannot wet it, and wind cannot dry it."
gem: My steady Self (unhurt at the core)
s: The cutting remark | During a quarrel, your close friend says, "No wonder nobody picks you." The words feel like a knife. That evening your elder sister says, "Words can hurt your feelings. They cannot cut what you are. And your friend will be sorry by tomorrow."

h: **Do not throw the same weapon back.** A sharp reply cuts the friendship further.
h: **Go to a friend or an elder who knows your worth** when words keep ringing in your head.
try: Think of one harsh thing someone said to you long ago, and notice that you are still whole.
bhakti: What God has placed in you is beyond the reach of any weapon. Rest in that when words sting.

## 2.24
sk: अच्छेद्योऽयमदाह्योऽयमक्लेद्योऽशोष्य एव च । नित्यः सर्वगतः स्थाणुरचलोऽयं सनातनः ॥
read: achchhedyo 'yam adaahyo 'yam akledyo 'shoshya eva cha | nityah sarva-gatah sthaanur achalo 'yam sanaatanah
t: Steady like a rock
one: The Self is everywhere, firm and unmoving, like a rock in a river that the water flows around.
meaning: "The Self cannot be cut, burned, wetted or dried. It is eternal, present everywhere, firm, unmoving and ever the same."
gem: My steady Self
s: The friend who stays the same | In your class, friendships change every term. One friend is the same with everyone, whether she tops the class or not, whether she is in a group or alone. You realise she is the person you trust most, and you wonder how to be like her.

## 2.25
sk: अव्यक्तोऽयमचिन्त्योऽयमविकार्योऽयमुच्यते । तस्मादेवं विदित्वैनं नानुशोचितुमर्हसि ॥

t: More than what can be seen
one: The most real part of you cannot be seen, measured or ranked, so do not grieve over the parts that can.
meaning: "The Self is said to be unseen, beyond thought and unchanging. Knowing this, you should not grieve."
gem: My steady Self (more than marks and looks)
s: The rank list | The rank list goes up, and you are in the bottom half. You feel as if the paper on the wall is a full description of you. Your friend, who topped, says, "That list does not know you made me laugh every day of my worst month."

## 2.26
sk: अथ चैनं नित्यजातं नित्यं वा मन्यसे मृतम् । तथापि त्वं महाबाहो नैवं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥
read: atha chainam nitya-jaatam nityam vaa manyase mritam | tathaapi tvam mahaa-baaho naivam shochitum arhasi
t: Even if you are not sure
one: Even if you are not sure about the big questions, sinking into sorrow still does not help.
meaning: "Even if you think the Self is born again and again and dies again and again, mighty Arjuna, you still should not grieve like this."
gem: Evenness (you do not need every answer to take the next step)
s: "What is the point of studying?" | Late one night your friend messages, "Why study at all? Nothing means anything." You do not have a clever answer. You write, "I don't know either. But come over tomorrow, and we will do the physics chapter together." He comes.
s: The big questions at 2 a.m. | At a sleepover on the terrace, you and your cousins argue till two about God, life and what happens after death. Nobody wins. In the morning you still have to fold the bedding and fetch the milk, and somehow that feels steadying.
s: A friend who has stopped praying | Your friend tells you she no longer believes in anything since her father fell ill. You do not preach. You walk home with her every day that week and ask about her father. Later she says the walks helped more than any argument.
h: **Let questions stay open.** It is fine to say, "I do not have this answer yet."
h: **Do the next useful thing anyway.** Wash, eat, study, show up for a friend.
h: **Take your big questions to an elder or a teacher you trust,** one at a time.
try: Write down one question you cannot answer, and next to it one small thing you can still do today.
bhakti: Krishna meets Arjuna where he is, doubts and all. We too can come to God before we have understood everything.

## 2.27
sk: जातस्य हि ध्रुवो मृत्युर्ध्रुवं जन्म मृतस्य च । तस्मादपरिहार्येऽर्थे न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि ॥
read: jaatasya hi dhruvo mrityur dhruvam janma mritasya cha | tasmaad aparihaarye 'rthe na tvam shochitum arhasi
t: What cannot be avoided
one: Some things cannot be avoided. Accepting them frees your strength for what you can still do.
meaning: "For one who is born, death is certain, and for one who has died, birth is certain. So you should not grieve over what cannot be avoided."
gem: Evenness (accepting what cannot be changed)
s: The friend who is moving away | Your best friend's father is transferred, and she will leave in a month. You spend the first week angry and sad. Then you both make a list of ten things to do together before she goes, and the month becomes one of your best.
s: The end of the summer | Every year you spend the summer with your cousins at your grandparents' house. In the last week everyone keeps saying, "Let us not talk about it." One cousin finally says, "It is ending whether we talk or not. So let us plan how we will stay in touch." You fix a call for the first Sunday of every month.

h: **Share the sadness with a friend or family member** who is going through the same ending.
try: Think of one thing that is ending soon, and plan one good thing to do before it does.
bhakti: Beginnings and endings are both held in God's hands. Trusting Him with what we cannot change brings rest.

## 2.28
sk: अव्यक्तादीनि भूतानि व्यक्तमध्यानि भारत । अव्यक्तनिधनान्येव तत्र का परिदेवना ॥

# Part 3: Your part to play (2.31–2.38)
intro: Krishna now speaks about duty. At every stage of life there is a part that is yours to play: as a student, at home, in your neighbourhood and towards yourself. Doing it with courage is better than running from it.

## 2.31
sk: स्वधर्ममपि चावेक्ष्य न विकम्पितुमर्हसि । धर्म्याद्धि युद्धाच्छ्रेयोऽन्यत्क्षत्रियस्य न विद्यते ॥
read: sva-dharmam api chaavekshya na vikampitum arhasi | dharmyaad dhi yuddhaach chhreyo 'nyat kshatriyasya na vidyate
t: Your own duty
one: Look at what your own duty is right now, and do not waver from it.
meaning: "Looking at your own duty too, you should not waver. For a warrior, there is nothing better than a fight for what is right."
gem: Mindful action (knowing my part)
s: More than what you were asked | Your mother asks you to lay the table, and you do it. Then you notice that the dustbin is full and the clothes are still on the line with rain coming. Nobody has asked you. You bring the clothes in and take the dustbin out. A home is not a hotel where you are a guest. You are part of it, and its work is partly yours.
s: The wrapper on the ground | After the match, you and your friends finish your snacks, and the wrappers stay on the ground. "Nobody else picks theirs up," one of them says. That is true. You pick up yours anyway, and then a few more, and walk to the bin. You do it because it is the right thing to do, whatever anyone else does. One friend quietly follows you.
s: Charging the phone, not yourself | During the holidays you stay up past midnight with shows, eat chips in place of lunch and do not step outside for days. You feel dull and cross. Your father says to you, "My girl, you charge your phone every night without fail. Who is charging you?" You begin with one thing: lights off at ten.

h: **At home, be part of the home.** Do the chores you are asked to do, notice what else needs doing, and offer to help.
h: **Outside, keep the places you use clean.** Do it because it is right, not because others are doing it.
h: **Look after yourself every day.** Eat healthy food, keep your body and mind fit, and keep your cravings for food and entertainment in hand. Too much of either is not good for you.
try: Write one duty you have as a student, one at home, one to your neighbourhood and one to yourself. Do one of them today without being asked.
bhakti: Each of us has been given a place and a part. Doing it well is a way of saying thank you to the One who gave it.

## 2.33

## 2.34
sk: अकीर्तिं चापि भूतानि कथयिष्यन्ति तेऽव्ययाम् । सम्भावितस्य चाकीर्तिर्मरणादतिरिच्यते ॥

## 2.35

s: The debate you skipped | You pull out of the debate, saying you have a cold. Your friend, who was your partner, knows it was nerves. He is not angry, only disappointed. You wish you had told him the truth and asked for help.
s: The festival at your friend's house | Your friend has invited you home for the festival, and you said yes. An hour before, you message, "Sorry, I can't come." The real reason is that the house will be full of people you do not know, your friend will be busy, and you are afraid you will have nobody to talk to. Then you tell her the truth. She replies, "Come half an hour early and help me arrange the lamps. By the time the others arrive, you will know half of them." You go.
s: The solo you passed on | Your music teacher offers you the solo in the next programme, and you suggest another student. Later your mother asks why. You realise you gave away a chance out of fear and called it modesty.
h: **Ask honestly, "Is this fear or good sense?"** Good sense has reasons. Fear has excuses.
h: **Tell a friend the real reason.** Fear shrinks when it is spoken.
h: **Take a friend along** for the first hard step.
try: Think of one thing you avoided recently. Write the real reason, just for yourself.
bhakti: Krishna knows Arjuna is brave, and He will not let fear decide for him. God sees our courage even when we have lost sight of it.

## 2.36
sk: अवाच्यवादांश्च बहून्वदिष्यन्ति तवाहिताः । निन्दन्तस्तव सामर्थ्यं ततो दुःखतरं नु किम् ॥
read: avaachya-vaadaamsh cha bahoon vadishyanti tavaahitaah | nindantas tava saamarthyam tato duhkhataram nu kim
t: When people talk
one: People will talk, and it hurts. Do not let their talk decide what you do.
meaning: "Those who wish you ill will say many unkind things and mock your ability. What could be more painful than that?"
gem: Evenness (not ruled by what people say)
s: The laughing group | You answer wrongly in class, and a group at the back laughs and repeats it for days. You stop raising your hand. Your bench-mate says, "They laugh at everyone. If you stop answering, they win and you lose marks."
s: The friend who believed it | A girl in your class is possessive and jealous, and she wants your closest friend for herself. She tells your friend unkind things about you that are not true. Your friend believes all of it without checking with you or with anyone else, and she stops talking to you for good. It hurts more than anything said to your face. You do not spread stories in return. You tell your friend once, calmly, "That is not true, and you can ask me anything." Whether she comes back is not in your hands. How you behave is.
s: What the relatives say | You hear that some relatives say you won the prize "by luck." You want to prove them wrong at every family gathering. Your mother says, "Practise the way you always did. That is the only answer that lasts."
h: **Do not be shocked when people talk.** Almost everyone is talked about unkindly at some time, so it does not mean something is wrong with you.
h: **Count the voices fairly.** Two loud critics are not everyone.
h: **Answer with steady work,** not with arguments.
h: **Say the truth once, calmly,** and do not spread stories in return.
try: Write the names of three people whose opinion of you truly matters. Listen to them first.
bhakti: Even Arjuna would have been mocked. God's opinion of us does not rise and fall with what people say.

## 2.37
sk: हतो वा प्राप्स्यसि स्वर्गं जित्वा वा भोक्ष्यसे महीम् । तस्मादुत्तिष्ठ कौन्तेय युद्धाय कृतनिश्चयः ॥
read: hato vaa praapsyasi svargam jitvaa vaa bhokshyase maheem | tasmaad uttishtha kaunteya yuddhaaya krita-nishchayah
t: You gain either way
one: When you give an honest effort, you gain something whether you win or lose. So stand up and decide.
meaning: "If you fall, you will reach heaven. If you win, you will enjoy the earth. So stand up, Arjuna, with your mind made up."
gem: Mindful action (making up my mind)
s: The scholarship exam | You and your friend both want to try for a scholarship, but only a few get it. "What if we fail?" you ask. She says, "Then we will know the syllabus better than anyone else in class. Let's fill in the form."
s: The quiz you could not win | Your friends want to enter the district quiz. You are sure you will not win. You enter anyway, and you do not win. But you learn to think quickly on a stage, and you make two friends you still meet.
s: Asking to join | Your neighbours are forming a team, and you want to play. If you ask and they say yes, good. If they say no, at least they know you are keen, and they may call you next time. Seen that way, there is no bad result. You ask.
h: **List what you gain if it goes well, and what you gain if it does not.** There is usually something on both sides.
h: **Decide once.** A made-up mind saves a great deal of energy.
h: **Try together with a friend** when you can. Shared effort is lighter.
try: Pick one thing you have been unsure about, and decide by tonight.
bhakti: Krishna shows Arjuna that an honest effort offered to God is never wasted, whichever way it turns out.

## 2.38
sk: सुखदुःखे समे कृत्वा लाभालाभौ जयाजयौ । ततो युद्धाय युज्यस्व नैवं पापमवाप्स्यसि ॥
read: sukha-duhkhe same kritvaa laabhaalaabhau jayaajayau | tato yuddhaaya yujyasva naivam paapam avaapsyasi
t: Win or lose, the same
one: Treat joy and sorrow, gain and loss, winning and losing alike, and then give your best.
meaning: "Treat pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat as the same, and then get ready for the fight. In this way you will do no wrong."
gem: Evenness
s: Playing against your best friend | You and your best friend reach the badminton final from opposite sides. You are afraid that whoever loses will be hurt. Before the match you agree, "We both play our best, and afterwards we eat together, whatever the score." You do.
s: One got in, one did not | You and your friend both try for the choir. You get in, and she does not. You do not hide your news, and you do not show it off either. You sit with her while she plans what to try next, and the friendship holds.
s: The team that lost | Your street's team practises for a month and loses its first match. The next match is a week away. Your captain says, "Same effort as last time. The result is not ours to fix." You find it easier to begin this time.
h: **Before you start, accept both results.** Say, "I may win, I may lose, and I will be all right."
h: **Keep the friendship above the score.**
h: **Give the same effort** after a loss as after a win.
try: Before your next test or match, say to yourself, "My job is the effort."
bhakti: A mind that stays even in gain and loss can offer everything to God. This verse opens the way to the teaching that follows.
