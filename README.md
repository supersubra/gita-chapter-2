# Bhagavad Gita, Chapter 2: a readable edition for young people

All 72 verses of Chapter 2, each with the Sanskrit, a reading guide, the meaning in simple words, three real-life moments, steps that help and a bhakti thought.

- `docs/index.html` is the edition to read in order.
- `docs/finder.html` is the same edition with a list for finding a verse by what is on your mind.
- `content/` holds the verse text, and `build_site.py` rebuilds both pages from it.

The Sanskrit follows the Gita Press, Gorakhpur edition. The real-life moments are illustrations, not case studies.
